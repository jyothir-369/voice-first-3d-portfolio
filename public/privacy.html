Privacy notice

Plain language. Short version: your microphone audio is never recorded or stored by this site.

Your voice

This site never records or stores audio. When you tap to talk, your browser's speech recognition converts your speech to text. In Chrome and Edge that conversion uses the browser vendor's speech service, which may receive your audio. This site cannot see or keep that audio.

Your questions

Your question text is sent to the assistant endpoint so it can generate an answer. This site does not log, store, or analyse question text beyond what is needed to produce a single reply.

Analytics

This site does not use any analytics service.

Third parties

  • Hosting provider — serves the site.
  • AI provider (Groq) — receives your question text to write an answer.
  • Speech service — the browser vendor's service converts speech to text.

Your choices

You can use every part of this site without a microphone: tap the question chips or the "Skip voice, use text" option. To ask any privacy question, email jyothirraghavalu369@gmail.com.

AI honesty

Replies come from an AI assistant and may contain mistakes. Spoken answers are generated, not pre-recorded. If something is wrong, email the address above and I will correct it.
